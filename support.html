Frequently Asked Questions

After signing in, click Report Lost Item from your dashboard or the homepage. Fill in a detailed description including colour, brand, size, and any unique markings. Upload a photo if you have one — this significantly improves our AI matching speed. Select the location and date last seen, then submit. You'll receive a confirmation email and real-time alerts when a match is found.
FindBack's AI analyses text descriptions, uploaded images, location data, and time patterns to compare your lost item report against all found item reports. It uses computer vision to match visual features and natural language processing to understand descriptions, achieving a 98% match accuracy rate. You receive a confidence score with every match suggestion.
Institutions can register by clicking Create Account and selecting the Institution tab. Choose your institution type (university, hospital, airport etc.), select a subscription plan, and complete the verification steps. Once approved, your institution gets a dedicated dashboard, staff management portal, and a customised lost & found queue for your members and visitors.
Yes! Institution portals support two access tiers: staff (who manage the queue and verify claims) and public visitors (who can submit found/lost reports using institution-specific credentials provided at the front desk). Institutions can also generate temporary access links for visitors.
When AI flags a potential match, you'll get an alert. Review the found item details and click Claim This Item. You'll be asked to provide ownership proof such as a serial number, receipt, or a unique identifier. The institution or finder reviews your claim — usually within 24 hours. Once approved, you arrange pickup through the platform's secure messaging system.
Absolutely. FindBack uses 256-bit AES encryption for all stored data and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. We are fully compliant with Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019. Your personal information is never sold to third parties. You can request a full data export or account deletion at any time from your profile settings.
The Free plan includes up to 5 item reports per month, basic AI matching, email alerts, and access to the public item search database. It's ideal for individuals who occasionally need to track lost personal belongings. For institutions or frequent users, our Standard and Premium plans offer unlimited reports, SMS alerts, staff portals, and advanced analytics.
You can browse the public search database without an account. However, reporting items, receiving AI match alerts, and claiming found belongings all require a free registered account. Registration takes under two minutes and gives you access to the full platform.
